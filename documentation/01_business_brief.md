# Business Brief

## Situation
An airline runs a tiered loyalty programme (Star, Nova, Aurora). Members earn points on flights and redeem them for travel. The loyalty team wants to know who is most valuable, why members leave, and whether the points scheme is sound.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| Loyalty programme manager | Which tier benefits to change | Activity, churn and value by tier |
| Retention (CRM) lead | Whom to contact before they cancel | Churn by segment, and any early warning signal |
| Finance partner | How large the points liability is | Points accumulated, redeemed and redemption value |
| Marketing lead | Whether to repeat the 2018 promotion | Promotion vs standard enrolments on value and churn |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports | What would change the decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How do flight activity and points differ by tier? | Whether tier benefits drive travel | A clear difference in flights per member-month between tiers |
| Q2 | Where is churn concentrated? | Which segments to target | A segment well above average churn, with enough members to be reliable |
| Q3 | What drives member value (CLV)? | Which profiles to grow | A driver with a large gap in CLV per month of tenure |
| Q4 | How do points earned and redeemed compare? | Whether to review the points scheme | A redemption ratio far below expectation |
| Q5 | Does activity change before a member cancels? | Whether an early-warning signal exists | A consistent drop before cancellation, compared with members who stay |
| Q6 | Do 2018 promotion members behave differently? | Whether to repeat the promotion | A difference that holds once tenure is accounted for |
| Q7 | How concentrated is member value? | How much to rely on top members | A top-10% share far above 10% |

## Scope
- **In:** flight activity (2017 to 2018), membership and cancellation dates, points, redemption value, CLV.
- **Out:** fare revenue, cost per flight, service complaints, and causes of cancellation.

## Constraints
- Two years of flight data only.
- Several records fall outside a member's membership dates. These are flagged and excluded from membership analyses (see the data and quality document).
